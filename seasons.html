Seasons · 四季

A street that keeps literary time时光作意

The neighbourhood's cultural programmes run on a four-season rhythm — one theme per season, each with its own colour, its own events, and its own way of using the street.

SPRING 春 · 诗书季

Books & Poetry Season

Readings, shared book tables and walks that pair the street's bookshop with its literary past. Spring is when the street re-reads itself.

SUMMER 夏 · 夜多伦

Night Duolun

Old film outdoors, low evening lighting along the lane, and weekend evening markets. The street stays up late in summer.

AUTUMN 秋 · 成诗季

The Autumn Art & Poetry Season

The year's main season: installation art along the street, poetry co-creation — visitors' lines go up on walls the same day — artist residencies, and collaborations that put new work into shopfronts.

WINTER 冬 · 暖多伦

Warm Duolun

Community winter rituals: scarves knitted for the street's statues, new-year markets, and letters written to the street itself.

The oral-history series: “Friends of Duolun Road”

Running through the year is a quiet, durable project: an oral-history series recording the people of the street — collectors, craftspeople, long-time residents, local historians — one person, one episode, numbered and archived. It is the street's memory being made while you watch. Selections appear in exhibitions and community events; the archive grows monotonically, which is exactly the point.

Now on: the Grand Shanghai 1933 exhibition

From September through December 2026, a community venue just off the street hosts an exhibition of old-Shanghai objects and documents drawn from a collection assembled over more than two decades — everyday objects, photographs and papers that map how the neighbourhood actually lived. Free to visit; small-group sessions on announced dates.

Programme details — dates, venues, sign-ups — are announced through the street's community channels in Chinese. This page describes the rhythm of the year so visitors know what kind of street they are walking into; check locally for what's on during your visit.
