Shops & Galleries · 店与画廊

Browse, don't rush慢慢逛

Duolun Road trades in patience: art, antiques, books, instruments, tea. Most shops are small, owner-run, and happy to talk. The selection below is a starting map, not a boundary — the street rewards wandering into whatever door is open.

No. 165JADE & STONE

Yuyuxuan 玉语轩

A quiet studio of jade and stone carving — the street's classic antique-and-craft register in miniature.

No. 173BOOKS

Mingzhu Bookstore 名著书屋

The street's bookshop: second-hand volumes, classics and local history. On a street of writers, the bookshop is the flagship species.

No. 177CRAFT

Cord-craft studio 绳编工作室

Hand-knotted decorative cordwork — an old folk craft practised at studio pace.

No. 179–181PAINTING

Hushan Painting Studio 虎山画苑

Traditional painting gallery and studio; exhibitions rotate through the year.

No. 181MEMORABILIA

Grand Shanghai 1933 大上海1933

An exhibition hall of old-Shanghai objects and documents, assembled over decades by a local collector — one family's archive of the street's material memory.

No. 189CERAMICS

Haicitang 海瓷堂

A collector's gallery of ceramics and sea porcelain, one of the street's senior businesses.

No. 191MUSIC

Zhiyin Guqin Studio 知音古琴

Studio and shop for the guqin, the seven-stringed zither of the literati. If someone is playing, stop and listen — it is the exact sound the street's residents made eight centuries apart.

No. 199ANTIQUES

Yiyuange 忆远阁

Antiques and collectibles with a Republican-era accent.

On-streetGALLERY

Shengdian Gallery 盛典画廊

An art gallery spread over three floors, open to cross-genre collaborations.

On-streetTEA

Zhenji Tea 珍记茶号

A tea merchant with a younger following — good for a restorative mid-walk cup.

On-streetCRAFT

Leather-craft studio 素人皮具

Hand-made leather goods, run by one of the street's most online-savvy makers.

On-streetDINING

Darlock Restaurant 达洛克餐厅

Mid-to-upscale dining for when the walk turns into an afternoon.

Pair the directory with two stops from the landmarks page: coffee inside the 1920s villa at the Old Film Café (No. 123), and contemporary art at the Duolun Museum of Modern Art (No. 27).

This is a curated selection of publicly trading businesses, listed by name, door number and trade only. Opening hours change — check locally on arrival. We deliberately publish no personal or contact details of shop owners.
