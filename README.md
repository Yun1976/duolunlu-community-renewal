## What this is

Duolun Road (多伦路) is a 550-metre historic street in Hongkou District, Shanghai,
designated a "Culture Celebrity Street" in 1999 and largely untouched since. By
2026 it had become what the local team calls *a fossil that could not be pried
open*: a strong heritage narrative, real foot traffic, and no mechanism connecting
residents, merchants, the property owner, and the street office.

This repository documents one year of work by the **Sichuan North Road Sub-district
community placemaking team (川造社)** on that street — the method, the mechanisms,
the six public activities, and the findings, including one counter-intuitive result
about media coverage.

It is published as an **open case library entry**: cities everywhere have good
renewal precedents; this is ours, in enough detail to be argued with, copied, or
criticised. Nothing here is a template. Everything here is annotated with the
limits of what it can support.

## The method in five lines

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A["1 · Ice-breaking<br/>conversation before survey"] --> B["2 · Diagnosis<br/>ballot on five issues"]
  B --> C["3 · Emergence<br/>paths nobody designed"]
  C --> D["4 · Integration<br/>resource library + PDCA"]
  D --> E["5 · Cultivation<br/>tiered, long-term"]
  E -. "next cycle" .-> A
```

1. **Ice-breaking** — market-embedded research, neighbourhood-committee sessions, door-to-door merchant interviews. Success metric: information barriers gone, not data collected.
2. **Diagnosis** — a multi-party revitalisation clinic, then a ballot on five issues (management, merchants, space, footfall, positioning). The ballot surfaced consensus; it did not choose a plan.
3. **Emergence** — merchant self-organisation, university–government–enterprise collaboration, and low-intervention "acupuncture" upgrades, none of them designed in advance.
4. **Integration** — an eight-dimension resource library, a six-step PDCA loop, and a toolkit (property map, merchant ledger, action plan, design drawings, cultural and creative matrix).
5. **Cultivation** — tiered commercial development, a merchant-alliance governance layer, and ongoing in-place cultural programming.

## Two findings worth stealing

**Reporting is not discussion.** The campaign produced 30+ items across 15+ outlets, yet verifiable engagement was thin: 1,013 reads across four government-portal articles, and an official Weibo post with zero likes. Meanwhile street-level user content about the same road reached 358,000 likes on a single post. The lesson: activity-specific content was scarce, so platforms discuss *the street*, not *the event*.

**Consensus grows from collaboration, not from structure.** On management — the issue with the least obvious answer — the ballot showed the strongest agreement: the governing body should emerge bottom-up from merchant collaboration, with the street office creating conditions rather than appointing a body.

## Data and boundaries

- No personal names, no shop door numbers, no property or renovation-intent records, no resident photographs. Individuals appear only as roles.
- Sample sizes in the field research were small (dozens of questionnaires, targeted interviews). Conclusions guide action; they do not support statistical inference.
- Two internal counts appear for the resource library. The cited number is **133 structured entries** — the eight per-dimension counts in the source deck sum exactly to 133. The other figure (204) includes survey artefacts that are not public material, so it is not used in citations. See the note in [the case](docs/case-en.md).
- All figures trace back to public reporting or platform pages collected by the team; data that cannot be verified publicly is marked as such.

## 中文摘要

多伦路是上海虹口区一条 550 米的历史文化街区，1999 年命名"文化名人街"，近三十年来未经历系统性更新。本仓库记录四川北路街道社区营造团队（川造社）2026 年在这条街上的工作：方法、机制、六场公共活动，以及一个反直觉的传播结论。

**五阶段方法**：破冰（市集嵌入式调研＋居委座谈＋商户逐户走访）→ 探索（活化诊断会＋五议题全民投票）→ 涌现（商户自发参与、政校企协同、针灸式业态活化）→ 整合（八维度资源库＋六环节 PDCA＋成套工具包）→ 培育（分层培育商业活力、共治体系、文化生长力）。

**两个值得抄走的发现**：

1. **被报道 ≠ 被讨论**。30+ 条报道、15+ 家媒体，可核验阅读量只有 1,013 次；而街区泛内容单条点赞可达 35.8 万——因为活动专属内容供给不足，平台讨论的是"街区"，不是"活动"。
2. **共识从协作中生长，不从结构中来**。在"管理之困"这个最难的问题上，投票反而呈现最强共识：治理主体应从商户协作中自下而上生长。

**边界**：不含真实姓名、门牌号、产权与改造意愿记录、居民照片；调研样本量小，结论用于指导动作而非统计推断；资源库对外统一口径为 **133 条结构化条目**（八维度分项相加即 133，可逐项核对），内部 204 条的总量口径含问卷/走访/反馈等不可公开材料，不对外引用。
