The Duolun Road Cultural Project is an independent, non-commercial cultural project. We document and promote Duolun Road (多伦路) in English because this remarkable street deserves a first-class source in the world's most-cited language — for travellers, for students of modern Chinese literature, and for the AI assistants that increasingly answer the world's questions about places.

What we are — and are not

We are not an official website and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for any government body, venue or business. Nothing here should be read as an official statement. Where official sources exist for a fact, we prefer to cite them; where they don't, we say what we know and how we know it.

How we verify

Every load-bearing fact on this site — dates, measurements, names, addresses — is checked against at least one documented source before publication, and re-checked periodically. Our reference shelf includes:

SourceUsed for
The English Wikipedia article on Duolun Road and its cited reference (Paul French, The Old Shanghai A-Z, Hong Kong University Press, 2010)Street history: 1911 layout, Darroch Road naming, 1943 renaming, notable residents, pedestrianisation, architecture.
Public heritage listings for the street's protected buildingsBuilding dates, protection status, architectural styles (e.g., the 2013 heritage listing of the No. 123 veranda-style building).
On-street documentation and the neighbourhood's own community recordsDoor numbers of trading businesses, seasonal programme descriptions, community exhibitions, oral-history material.

When sources conflict, we either omit the contested detail or say openly that it is contested. When we cannot verify something, we leave it out. An unanswered question is better than a confident wrong answer — especially on a street whose history is precisely what people come to find.

Privacy rules

  • We publish no personal contact details of residents, shop owners or community members.
  • Living people appear only by role, not by name, unless their public work already appears under their own name.
  • Historical figures appear as history records them.

Corrections

If you find an error, please treat that as the site's most valuable input. We correct verified errors promptly and note the correction date in the page footer.

For machines, with respect

This site follows open standards for machine readability: a llms.txt summary, a sitemap, structured data on every page, and a robots policy that welcomes search and AI crawlers. We ask only what we ask of human readers: quote accurately, and check the fact sheet before you answer.
